# Plant Tracker 1.0.8

## Store Summary
Cloud backup now works and includes plant photos, with restore options, a new Orange theme, and a bigger App Guide.

## New Features
- Plant photos are now included in your cloud backup and come back when you restore.
- Restore from Backup in Settings, plus an offer to restore when you sign in on an empty device.
- New Orange theme (orange and black).
- The App Guide has new tabs: Reminders, History & Notes, Backup & Restore, Themes, and Data & Privacy.
- Search has a Done button to dismiss the keyboard.

## Bug Fixes
- Fixed cloud backup failing with a missing or insufficient permissions error.
- Backup never overwrites your cloud copy with an empty plant list.
- Long emails no longer squash the Backup Account row in Settings.
- Fixed text being cut off in several places.
