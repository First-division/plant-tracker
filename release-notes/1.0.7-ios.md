# Plant Tracker 1.0.7

## Store Summary
Backdated watering, a friendlier first-run tour, clearer confirmations, and accessibility improvements.

## New Features
- Log a past watering with a date and time, and see it on the calendar and home screen.
- Choose a custom default watering interval (days, weeks or months) with a scroll wheel.
- Notifications are now offered during the welcome tour, and you can skip the tour or the name step.
- Pick a plant's birthday with a date picker, and remove a plant's photo.
- Undo a quick watering from the calendar.
- Search has a clear button, and the calendar has an empty state for new accounts.
- Plant gender options are now Male, Female, They/them and Other.

## Bug Fixes
- Tabs now use the native look and follow the app's light or dark setting.
- The default theme swatch now shows a black and white split.
- Delete, clear history and reset now explain what they remove, and reset shows progress and errors.
- Closing the plant form with unsaved changes now asks before discarding.
- A failed household or backup sign-in no longer leaves its switch stuck.
- Sign-in errors show on the sheet, and Cancel works while signing in.
- Larger touch targets, accessibility labels, and theme colors on modals.
